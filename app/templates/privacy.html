Privacy

ACKuifer collects as little as it can. This page says what that is and what happens to it.

What we collect

  • Your email address.
  • The neighborhoods you chose for alerts.
  • When you signed up, confirmed, and last used your subscription.
  • If you send feedback: your message, the page you sent it from, and your email address if you choose to give it.

We don't ask for your name, house number or phone number.

What we use it for

Only to send the alerts you asked for and messages about your subscription, and to read feedback you send. We never sell or share your information, and there are no ads.

Who handles it

  • Railway hosts the site and its database.
  • Resend delivers our emails.
  • Mapbox provides the map images.
  • Plausible counts visits to the site without cookies and without collecting personal data.

Cookies

The public site sets no cookies.

Unsubscribing

Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can change your neighborhoods with the "Manage subscriptions" link.

When you unsubscribe, we keep your address marked as unsubscribed so we never email you again by mistake. To have it deleted completely, write to {{ contact_email }}.
